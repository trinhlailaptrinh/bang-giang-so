Bảng Giảng Số

Write, cast to TV, take roll

Write with Apple Pencil, show only the board on the class TV, take attendance, pick students at random, make groups. Saves by class, fully offline.

Coming soon to the App Store

Bảng Giảng Số turns your iPad into the classroom board. Write with Apple Pencil, let students follow along on the TV or projector, and keep every lesson by class so you can pick up next time or send it to students who were absent. Attendance, calling on students, groups and deadlines live in the same app.

Write with apple pencil

  • Pen, highlighter, eraser, colors and widths, undo/redo.
  • Fingers scroll and zoom, so a stray touch never becomes a stroke.
  • Multi-page lessons: add, delete and reorder pages.
  • Saves continuously, with no Save button. Reopen a lesson and you are back on the page where you stopped.
  • Page backgrounds: plain, Vietnamese ruled notebook, grid, lined, blackboard and green board.
  • Ruler, basic shapes (line, arrow, rectangle, circle, triangle, coordinate axes) and a compass.
  • Page templates: coordinate grid, problem and solution, two columns, ruled page with a title.

Show only the board on the tv

  • Connect with an HDMI/USB-C cable or AirPlay: the TV shows only the board area of the current page.
  • Toolbars, the page list and your student list never appear on the TV.
  • Laser pointer with fading light trail, countdown timer with a bell, and a curtain you can slide down to reveal the board bit by bit.
  • Unplug mid-lesson and nothing is lost; plug back in and the TV shows the current page again.

Import pdfs and images to annotate

  • Import a PDF from Files: each PDF page becomes a board page you can write on.
  • Add a photo from Photos or Files to the current page, keeping its aspect ratio.
  • Your ink is stored separately, so the original document stays untouched.

Export pdf for students

  • Export the whole lesson as one PDF with backgrounds and handwriting.
  • Share through Mail, AirDrop, messaging apps or save it to Files.
  • Copy a lesson to another class when you teach the same material twice.

Classes and attendance

  • Create classes and add students one by one, paste a multi-line list, or import from an Excel/CSV file with a preview and duplicate warnings.
  • Attendance per session: everyone starts present; tap a name to mark excused or unexcused absence.
  • Monthly attendance stats, and export attendance to an Excel file.
  • Lessons listed by class, newest first.

Random names, groups, seating, stars

  • Random name picker: names shuffle, then the chosen student is shown big, on the TV too. Students absent today are skipped; nobody is called twice until the whole class has had a turn.
  • Random groups of the students who are present, by number of groups or people per group, reshuffle any time, shown on the TV.
  • Seating chart per class: drag students to seats, auto-arrange, tap to mark attendance. The picker shows where the student sits.
  • Star rewards: add or remove stars quickly during class, with weekly and monthly leaderboards.

To-dos and timetable

  • Track grading and reports with a deadline and an optional class; overdue items are flagged and done with one tap.
  • Local reminders before a deadline.
  • Weekly timetable: open the app during a period and it suggests the current class with a New Lesson button.

Settings and backup

  • Choose the app language (System, Vietnamese, English), light or dark appearance, default pen and background, sound and duration of the name picker.
  • Back up all your data to one file and restore it on another iPad.
