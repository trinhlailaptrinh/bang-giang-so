Privacy Policy – Bảng Giảng Số

Last updated: October 18, 2026

Publisher: NGUYEN NHUT TRINH ("we"). Contact: trinhnn1509x@gmail.com. Support: https://trinhlailaptrinh.github.io/bang-giang-so/support/. This policy is also published at https://trinhlailaptrinh.github.io/bang-giang-so/privacy/.

1. Summary

  • Bảng Giảng Số works without an account or sign-in.
  • We do not operate a server that receives your data, and we do not sell data.
  • The app has no ads and no third-party analytics or tracking tools.

2. Data the app stores

All data below is entered or created by you and is stored in the app's storage on your iPad.

DataContent
ClassesClass name, subject
StudentsStudent names you type or paste
AttendanceEach student's status per session (Present, Excused absence, Unexcused absence)
LessonsHandwriting and drawing strokes, board pages, assigned date and class
Imported filesPDFs and images you choose from Files or Photos to place on board pages
To-dosText and due dates for tasks such as grading or submitting reports

Student names and attendance records can be personal information about other people (students). See section 6.

3. When data leaves the device

As far as we know of the app, data is not sent by the app to our servers or to any third party. Data leaves the iPad only through actions you take or that the operating system performs:

  • Projecting to a TV/projector (HDMI/USB-C cable or AirPlay): only the writing-board area is shown on the external display. Your toolbar, page list, menus and notifications are not shown. Anyone who can see the display can see the board content.
  • Importing files: when you pick a PDF or image from Files (which may be on iCloud Drive or another provider) or from Photos, access to those sources is governed by Apple's or that provider's policies.
  • Apple backups: if you enable iCloud or computer backup for your iPad, the app's data may be included in that backup, which Apple manages.

4. Third parties

The app integrates no third-party advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or sign-in services. Apple services (App Store, AirPlay, iCloud, Files, Photos) are operated by Apple under Apple's policies. Apple may give developers aggregated, anonymous download and crash statistics through App Store Connect; we do not receive your class data through that channel.

5. Your rights and how to delete data

  • View and edit: you can view and edit all data inside the app.
  • Delete in parts: delete students, classes, lessons or to-dos in the app.
  • Delete everything: deleting the app from your iPad removes the data the app stores on the device. Any iCloud backup can be managed under Settings &gt; [your name] &gt; iCloud &gt; Manage Account Storage.
  • Because we hold no copy of your data on a server, we cannot retrieve, export or delete it for you. If you have questions about your rights under applicable data-protection law, contact us.

6. Children and student data

  • The app is for teachers and adults, not for children to use. Students only view content on the TV; they do not use the app.
  • We do not knowingly collect information from children. Student names stay on the teacher's device.
  • As the person entering the data, you are responsible for having an appropriate basis (for example, school rules) to store student names. We recommend entering only the minimum needed, such as given names.

7. Security

Data sits in the app's private storage on your iPad and is protected by iPadOS security features. Set a passcode on your iPad and keep the OS up to date. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

8. Changes

If the app changes how it handles data (for example, adding cloud sync), we will update this policy and its date before that feature is released.
