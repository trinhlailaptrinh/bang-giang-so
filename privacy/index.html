Privacy Policy – Bảng Giảng Số

Last updated: October 21, 2026

Publisher: NGUYEN NHUT TRINH ("we"). Contact: trinhnn1509x@gmail.com. Support: https://trinhlailaptrinh.github.io/bang-giang-so/vi/support/. This policy is also published at https://trinhlailaptrinh.github.io/bang-giang-so/vi/privacy/.

1. Summary

  • Bảng Giảng Số works without an account or sign-in.
  • The app works offline. We do not operate a server that receives your data, we do not sell data, and we do not send data to third parties.
  • The app has no ads and no analytics or tracking tools.
  • We do not collect email addresses, phone numbers, location, contacts or advertising identifiers.

2. Data the app stores

All data below is entered or created by you and is stored in the app's private storage on your iPad.

DataContent
ClassesClass name, subject
StudentsFull names you type, paste or import from an Excel/CSV file
AttendanceEach student's status per session (Present, Excused absence, Unexcused absence)
LessonsHandwriting and drawing strokes, board pages, page backgrounds, assigned date and class
Imported files and imagesPDFs and images you choose from Files or Photos to place on board pages
To-dosTitle, due date, related class, completion status
Seating charts, groups, reward starsSeat positions, per-session student groups, each student's star count
TimetableWeekday, period, start/end time, class
SettingsLanguage, appearance, default background and pen, sound and duration of the name-picker effect

Student names, attendance, seating and stars can be personal information about other people (students). See section 7.

3. Where data is stored

  • Only on your iPad, in the app's private database and file folder. iPadOS encrypts this area when the device is locked.
  • The developer holds no copy and cannot access this data.

4. When data leaves the device

The app does not send data to our servers or to any third party. Data leaves the iPad only through actions you take or that the operating system performs:

  • Projecting to a TV/projector (HDMI/USB-C cable or AirPlay): the display shows only the writing-board area. Your toolbar, page list, student list, attendance and menus are not shown. Content you choose to trigger does appear: the name (and seat) of a randomly picked student, group lists after you split groups, the timer and the laser pointer. Anyone who can see the display can see this content.
  • Exporting files: when you export a lesson PDF, export attendance to Excel or create a backup file, the app creates the file and opens the iOS share sheet. The file goes only where you choose (Zalo, Mail, AirDrop, Files…) and you keep and share it yourself. A backup file contains all the data in section 2 and is not separately encrypted by the app; keep it safe.
  • Importing files and photos: when you pick a PDF or Excel/CSV file from Files (which may be on iCloud Drive or another provider) or a photo from Photos, access to those sources is governed by Apple's or that provider's policies. Restoring a backup replaces the existing data in the app after you confirm.
  • Apple backups: the app's data is included in your iPad's iCloud or computer backup if you have those backups turned on. The backup belongs to your iCloud account and is managed by Apple; we cannot access it.

5. Permissions and notifications

  • Photos: the app uses the system photo picker. It receives only the photos you select and does not request access to your whole photo library.
  • Files: the app reads only the files you select through the document picker.
  • To-do reminders: when you first turn on reminders, the app asks for permission to send notifications. These are local notifications created and shown by the iPad itself, not through a server. They may contain the title of your to-do. You can turn this permission off at any time in iOS Settings.
  • Language: your language choice is stored in the app's settings on the device.

6. Third parties

The app integrates no third-party advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or sign-in services. Apple services (App Store, AirPlay, iCloud, Files, Photos, notifications) are operated by Apple under Apple's policies. Apple may give developers aggregated, anonymous download, usage and crash statistics through App Store Connect, only for users who agreed to share them; we do not receive your class data through that channel.

7. Children and student data

  • The app is for teachers and adults, not for children to use. Students only view content on the TV; they do not use the app.
  • We do not collect information from children. Student names stay on the teacher's device.
  • As the person entering the data, you are responsible for having an appropriate basis (for example, school rules or any required consent) to store student names. We recommend entering only the minimum needed.
  • When projecting, consider what the whole class can see, especially names and stars.

8. Your rights and how to delete data

  • View and edit: you can view and edit all data inside the app.
  • Delete in parts: delete students, classes, lessons or to-dos in the app. Deleting a class also deletes that class's students and attendance; its lessons move to "Unassigned" and are not deleted.
  • Export and backup: you export PDF, Excel or backup files yourself inside the app.
  • Delete everything: deleting the app from your iPad removes the data the app stores on the device. Files you exported or shared, and any iCloud backup, are outside the app and you must delete them yourself; manage backups under Settings > [your name] > iCloud > Manage Account Storage.
  • Because we hold none of your data, we cannot retrieve, export or delete it for you. If you have questions about your rights under applicable data-protection law, contact us.

9. Security

Data sits in the app's private storage on your iPad and is protected by iPadOS security features. Set a passcode on your iPad and keep the OS up to date. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

10. Changes

If the app changes how it handles data (for example, adding cloud sync), we will update this policy and its date before that feature is released.
